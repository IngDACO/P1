# Lift Installation — Full Task Breakdown (Stage → Activity → Task)

Third level added under the existing Stage → Activity structure. Tasks are drawn from the detail already captured in `Process_Knowledge_Reference.md` — where no further sub-detail has been given yet, the Activity is left as a single Task (marked *"no further breakdown yet"*) rather than inventing detail. This document will get more granular as the Process Knowledge doc is expanded.

No weights at Task level yet — that's a later step once the Activity-level weights are finalized.

---

## RIP-OUT TRACK

### R0 — Set Up
- **Receive toolbox for installer** *(no further breakdown yet)*
- **Receive rip-out kit** — tools/gear specific to rip-out, distinct from the Inex kit used later in install
- **Hoardings & protection**
  - Transport hoarding materials to site
  - Build hoardings on platform/concourse (timber) or cages (metal) — same task, different material depending on the job
  - Install compound & protection
  - Protect glass shaft panels (where present) — prevents cracking/breaking from falling debris during rip-out
  - Deactivate nearby smoke detector — near-universal on rip-outs in existing/occupied buildings
- **Build working deck(s)** — optional, job-specific temporary platform(s) in the shaft for rip-out access; not assumed on every job
- **General preparation of building/site/compound** *(no further breakdown yet)*
- **Other deliveries** *(no further breakdown yet)*

### R1 — Take Possession & Initial Shutdown
- **Take possession of lift**
  - Formal handover/sign-on to the existing lift
  - Initial site walk of the lift being ripped out
- **Initial electrical shutdown/isolation**
  - Shut down lift at main switch
- **Install top-of-shaft beam/hook point**
  - Drive old cabin in inspection mode to access top of shaft (only if no existing hook point)
  - Install beam/hook point structure — often supplied/completed via Schindler's supply chain (including Liftronic, a Schindler-acquired company — same lifts, no functional difference); can span multiple log entries including bolt follow-ups
  - Install SWL (Safe Working Load) rating plate on the beam

### R2 — Tirak Installation & Transfer to Temporary Drive
- **Position cabin at correct height** *(no further breakdown yet)*
- **Fully kill lift electrically / route mains power to tirak**
  - Install Inex kit orange box (temp 3-phase power box)
  - Connect mains to orange box
  - Kill lift at main switch (final, non-reversible for this phase)
- **Mount tirak** *(no further breakdown yet)*
- **Setup electric chain block** — secondary rigging tool, electric-powered, functions like a chain block; used in R3 for lifting/lowering the car and landing the CW tank. Confirmed general-purpose (Danilo, Sep 2026) — also used during Installation (e.g., lifting the motor into place). Minifor is a distinct device serving the same secondary-lifting-aid role, mounted alongside the tirak.
- **Attach tirak to cabin** *(no further breakdown yet)*
- **Hang tirak on hook point** *(no further breakdown yet)*
- **Drive up with tirak, collect cabin on temporary motor** *(no further breakdown yet)*
- **Release pressure from old ropes/belts OR detach piston**
  - Traction lifts: release ropes/belts tension
  - Hydraulic lifts: detach piston
- **Confirm free movement under tirak only, lift fully isolated/dead**
  - "Verify lift fully isolated/dead" is usually implied by orange box connection; log only if explicitly mentioned
- **Lighten cabin if too heavy for intended tirak ratio**
  - Strip components to reduce weight ("make lift as light as possible")
  - If lightening isn't enough, upgrade reeving ratio (e.g. 2:1 → 4:1) rather than proceed underpowered

Note: R2 and former R3 (Transfer to Temporary Drive) merged — rarely evidenced as a distinct step in real logs, reads as one continuous activity.

### R3 — Full Rip-Out
- **Rip out mechanical components — traction**
  - Remove rails & rings (car + CW) — also applies to hydraulic lifts, which have rails too
  - Remove cabin (chain block used for controlled lifting/lowering, if set up in R2)
  - Remove CW/CW tank (chain block used for landing the CW tank)
  - Remove landing doors (unless client wants sills/frames retained)
  - Remove pit components
- **Rip out mechanical components — hydraulic**
  - Drain/empty oil tank in machine room (MR)
  - Remove hose running through shaft wall, connected to ram
  - Pump remaining oil out of ram/hose
  - Remove ram/piston
  - Remove hydraulic structure
- **Rip out electrical components**
  - Remove wiring
  - Remove controller/boxes
- **Retain/preserve components for reuse** — conditional, only if client requests. Originally logged as sills/door frames specifically, but generalizes to any component (e.g. superstructure/frame) — doesn't affect removal of anything else.
- **Final clear-out / site prepared for new install**
  - Remove scrap/debris
  - Sweep/clean shaft, pit, machine room

Note: watch for tool-access blockers here — a job can stall from a needed tool being locked away with a colleague on a different job, distinct from consumable (bolt/packer) shortages.

## MECHANICAL INSTALLATION

### Stage 1 — Set Up
- **Receive toolbox** *(no further breakdown yet)*
- **Receive Inex kit** *(no further breakdown yet)*
- **Receive lift delivery**
  - Unload delivery
  - Count/verify boxes against manifest
  - Check box codes (multi-lift sites, avoid mixing kits)
- **Paperwork**
  - Hand-over/procedure forms signed
  - SWMS sign-on
  - (Note: site induction is NOT counted here — see Logistics & Site Support, non-progress)
- **Snap test (trimmer beam/hook point load test)** — performed at the very start of the job; doing it later is a mistake and a safety risk. Required on full new installs as well as rip-outs (see R1). Also called a "pull test."
- **Builders lift protection & handover** — conditional, job-specific
  - Install interior protection (floor, walls, ceiling)
  - Protect landing door frames at every level
  - Hand over to builder once construction hoist is replaced
- **Hang temporary work lighting** *(no further breakdown yet)*
- **Compound/Hoardings**
  - Transport hoarding materials to site
  - Build hoardings on platform/concourse
  - Install compound & protection
- **Install sky climber** — conditional, only on jobs using a false car; works like a motor
  - Install rope
  - Connect power
  - Install pendant (control)
- **Install sky lock** — conditional, only on jobs using a false car; works like a speed governor
  - Install rope
  - Attach directly to safety gear

### Stage 2 — Prepping
Note: prep is frequently not logged as a separate step. If a log shows a ring/rail/door (or any other component requiring prep) successfully installed, treat prep as implicitly complete for that component even with no explicit "prep" entry — don't withhold Stage 2 credit for lack of a direct log line.

- **Clean rails** *(no further breakdown yet — remove protective grease)*
- **Clean fishplates** *(no further breakdown yet)*
- **Install fishplates to rails (male side)** *(no further breakdown yet)*
- **Prep doors** — panels/blades, frames
- **Prep cabin walls** *(no further breakdown yet)*
- **Prep cabin doors** *(no further breakdown yet)*
- **Prep cabin reveals** *(no further breakdown yet)*
- **Prep COP** *(no further breakdown yet)*
- **Prep sills** — includes stripping/peeling protective film or packaging off new sills ("strip landing sills" = this task, not removal)
- **Prep landing door headers** *(no further breakdown yet)*
- **Prep cabin panels** *(no further breakdown yet)*
- **Prep cabin header** *(no further breakdown yet)*
- **Assist concrete cutting** — support role, door opening prep
- **Fire services access & assist** — dedicate time for the fire subcontractor to enter the shaft and install the water sprinkler/smoke detector; sometimes includes roof-of-car access so they can core-drill holes to run their pipes/cabling. Confirmed as a standard part of every new-install job (Danilo, Sep 2026), distinct from groutguarding/fire trims.

Note (Danilo, Aug 2026): on an Installation job, "strip" means prepping (peeling protective film/packaging off a new component), not removal. Only read "strip"/"remove" as actual removal on a Rip-Out job, or when the component was clearly already installed.

Note (Danilo, Sep 2026): **on-site bracket cutting** isn't a standard prep step — it's a recurring practical workaround crews use to solve fit/alignment problems as they come up (same general category as the hollow-brick-substrate/custom-fabricated-bracket pattern in the Process Knowledge doc). Not its own task line; log it in context rather than as routine prep.

### Stage 3 — Plumbing & Survey
- **Set plumb template** *(no further breakdown yet)* — or **set laser plumb** as an alternate method (laser point in the pit, no template needed at top, quicker overall). Same activity/task either way.
- **Build frame for template** — always at top of shaft
- **Mount plumbline frame + template** *(no further breakdown yet)*
- **Throw plumblines, attach weights, let settle**
  - Attach weights to plumblines
  - Let settle under gravity
- **Record measurements on wall** — reference points for later
- **Install pit plumb brackets** *(no further breakdown yet)*
- **Adjust pit brackets to gravity reference** — back to recorded wall measurements
- **Survey** — verify planned position vs. existing shaft
- **Remove plumb template/frame** *(no further breakdown yet)*

### Stage 4 — Car Assembly
- **Install first 2 rings** — 1 omega bracket (also called "combination bracket"/"combo bracket") + 1 single bracket, per Schindler
- **Install first 2 car rails** *(no further breakdown yet)*
- **Install car guide shoes** *(no further breakdown yet)*
- **Install U/Wing brackets** — used specifically for platform/yoke assembly
- **Install yoke/base, level it** *(no further breakdown yet)*
- **Install platform** — also called a "cradle" *(no further breakdown yet)*
- **Throw fillerweights** — into the CW tank (not the car platform — clarified, Danilo Sep 2026). Easiest right after platform install, before cabin walls go up, but not strictly sequential — can go in any time up through right before belting; ~50% of the tank needs to be filled by belting time to balance the cabin's weight.
- **Install cabin sill** *(no further breakdown yet)*
- **Build cabin (walls)** *(no further breakdown yet — includes the "reverses," the front walls forming the door opening)*
- **Install cabin doors** *(no further breakdown yet)* — distinct from the walls/reverses and from the header
- **Install cabin ceiling** — the "roof," same task either term is used
- **Install uprights** — two structural frame members, part of cabin assembly (not a separate "superstructure")
- **Install top bow** — joins the two uprights together at the top
- **Install cabin header (mechanical)** *(no further breakdown yet)*
- **Install COP (mechanical)** *(no further breakdown yet)*
- **Install cabin interior handrails/kickplates/bumpers** *(no further breakdown yet)*
- **Attach tirak + stopblock**
  - Attach tirak
  - Attach stopblock/blockstop — **temporary device** (corrected from earlier "permanent" assumption), removed later once rails reach the top (see Stage 6)
- **Attach speed governor rope to cabin** — the far end of the same rope runs to the tensioner device ("Jenny wheel") pinned to the rail in the pit (Stage 8); the governor device itself sits on the single/Z-side bedplate (Stage 6). One system, three install points.
- **Quick-calibrate safety gripper (initial)** — exception to the general "tune verb → Stage 14" rule; happens right after the cabin is first installed, then gets properly recalibrated later at full commissioning (Stage 14)
- **Install spear** — door interlock mechanism, cabin header side
- **Install rooftop handrail** *(no further breakdown yet)*
- **Install mirror** — conditional, only if job spec includes one
- **Install cabin toe guard** — confirmed as its own real, weighted activity (Danilo, Sep 2026); separate physical component from the landing toe guards/skirts in Stage 7 (Landing Doors); same name, different piece, mounted under the cabin sill rather than the landing sill
- **Earth cabin platform** — confirmed as its own real, weighted activity (Danilo, Sep 2026); a log entry that reads like someone double-checking whether platform earthing was already done maps here, not to rework

### Stage 5 — Counterweight (CW) Assembly
- **Install first 2 CW rails** *(no further breakdown yet)*
- **Install CW guide shoes** *(no further breakdown yet)*
- **Install props** *(no further breakdown yet)*
- **Install CW tank**
  - Install CW dumbbell — the point on top of the CW tank where the belts connect and pick up the CW
  - Install CW belt keepers — sit on top of the CW tank, surrounding the dumbbell; keep the belts contained and clear of the installer for safety

### Stage 6 — Shaft Climb & Bedplates
- **Install rings to top** — repeat ring pattern up shaft. "Gauge" (e.g. "gauge top level/ring") is installer shorthand for calibrating rail brackets — part of this task, not a separate one.
- **Install car rails to top** — "finals" = the final/top rail lengths cut to size before installing here
- **Install CW rails to top** — same "finals" note applies
- **Install motor bedplate** — also called MBB (confirmed abbreviation for motor bedplate). Pinned to the rails as part of installation, same fastening method as the governor tension device pin in the pit (Stage 8).
  - Install Hilti plates — anchor/mounting plates (Hilti = fastener brand) used for the motor install (Danilo, Sep 2026)
  - Install motor
  - Install bottles (belt-holding brackets)
- **Install single bedplate** — also called governor bedplate, or Z side bedplate (named for the Z-shaped single-side bracket it sits against). Also pinned to the rails.
  - Install speed governor — includes plastic cover, standard on all Schindler speed governors
  - Install salsis tape — includes salsis bracket (mounting hardware for the unit)
  - Install bottles
  - Install KSS switches — safety switches *(no further breakdown yet)*
- **Seal machine room / shaft penetrations** — leftover/code-required holes between MR and shaft, common on nearly every project. "Filled sump hole" = same task, not separate.

Note (Danilo, Sep 2026): the stopblock (attached in Stage 4, alongside the tirak) stays in place through the shaft climb — it does **not** get removed here. Removal happens later, alongside tirak removal — see Stage 13, "Remove stopblock."

### Stage 7 — Landing Doors
- **Install rots** — install-assist bars
- **Install sills** *(no further breakdown yet)*
- **Install frames** *(no further breakdown yet)*
- **Install headers** *(no further breakdown yet)*
- **Cut/expand concrete door openings** — routine prep is normal; occasionally an opening is undersized for the new lift and needs a "chaser job" (cutting/expanding concrete). This is a design-side error, not an installer error, and uncommon — worth flagging distinctly from routine cutting if it shows up in a log.
- **Groutguarding** — metal sheeting over penetrations/concrete opening around frame; fire-rated, also called "fire brackets," "fire trims," "fire rating," or "fire seal" (Danilo, Sep 2026, confirmed same job); uses black grout tape as part of the material
- **Install door panels** *(no further breakdown yet)*
- **Install skirts** — also called toe guards; the plate at the edge, under the landing sill. A separate physical component from cabin toe guards (Stage 4, Car Assembly) — same name, different piece.
- **Install keepers** — lock keeper plates (also appears as a tuning-phase entry, see Stage 14)
- **Install door weights** — weights/strings providing constant closing force
- **Bridge landing door circuit** — temporary electrical bridge, allows doors to be tested/operated before full wiring is complete. Confirmed Installation-track activity, not rip-out, even when logged on an early/rip-out-adjacent day.
- **Tune doors**
  - Tune headers
  - Tune panels
  - Tune contacts
  - (rollers are tuned here too in practice but tracked under Stage 14 per the interpretation rule)

### Stage 8 — Pit Mechanical
- **Install ladder** *(no further breakdown yet)*
- **Install/trim buffers (car + CW)**
  - Install car buffer
  - Install CW buffer
  - Trim for precise running clearance (prevents CW tank striking top structure, and vice versa)
- **Install governor tension device** — tension sheave/pulley, tail sheave, jockey pulley, "Jenny wheel" (pit-mounted rope tensioner for the speed governor); pinned to the rail as part of installation, on every job
- **Install CW screen** — segregation barrier between CW tank and rest of pit
- **Chisel pit** — concrete cutting/chiseling for leveling or sump-related adjustments

## ELECTRICAL INSTALLATION

### Stage 9 — Headroom Wiring
- **Install electrical boxes**
  - AED (optional) — also logged as "AESD," same box
  - VAF/drive
  - Option box (security) — the connection point between the lift and the building: a flex cable runs out from the box to the cabin on the lift side, while all building-supplied services (cameras, swipe/card readers, and any other device the building wants integrated with the lift) land on the box from the building side
  - Cube (connectivity)
  - Pixel (intercom, also internet-connected — not interchangeable with cube)
  - Switch JH
  - Note: the drive unit needs clearance from the lift's actual travel path — installed too close, it can end up in collision range and require relocation as rework
- **Wire all boxes** *(no further breakdown yet)* — includes controller wiring specifically, which belongs here (Stage 9) even when logged near shaft wiring entries (Danilo, Sep 2026)
- **Bottles contact (belt/rope monitoring)** *(no further breakdown yet)*
- **Spin motor** — test run once wiring complete

### Stage 10 — Cabin Wiring
**Under cabin**
- Weight sensors
- Alarms
- Safety gear contacts
- Speaker
- Traveller/flat/flex cables

**Inside cabin**
- COP wiring — down to underneath, up to OKR
- Ceiling decoration wiring — lights, fans
- Fire switch (COP-side, keyed) — one system with a matching landing-side install; see Stage 11 Door wiring

**Top of cabin**
- OKR box — terminates wiring from all three cabin zones, including main + optional flex cables

Note: a log entry that just says "flex cable" with no zone named usually means both the Under-cabin and Top-of-cabin flex cable tasks were done — credit both.
- Salsis reader
- Magnet reader — install/prep entries land here; "tuned magnet reader" entries go to Stage 14 instead
- Wiring from cabin header/PDO
- Lightrays — door safety sensors
- Top-of-car emergency light
- Speaker
- Flex cables
- Rooftop handrail contact — conditional, only when handrail is foldable

### Stage 11 — Shaft Wiring
**Door wiring**
- LIP/LOP cables — landing display cabling
- Door locks — safety contacts
- Call button wiring
  - Chase (prep the concrete for call button installation)
- Fire switch (landing call-button-box side, keyed) — one system with a matching COP-side install; see Stage 10 Inside cabin

**Line**
- Contact cable run to pit
- Power cable for lights
- Lights
- Install cable tray — cable management infrastructure, distinct from the wiring itself
- Install magnet flags — conditional, only where landing door headers don't come with magnets built in. Small rounded magnets mounted on the rails, ~8mm from the cabin-mounted magnet reader (Stage 10, Top of cabin). If headers DO come with integrated magnets, the reader instead mounts on the cabin door header — same 8mm gap, no separate rail-mounted flags needed.

Note: "ducting" is a protective covering over this same wiring (common on glass shaft jobs), not a separate task.

### Stage 12 — Pit Wiring
- Stop button box — pit ladder
- Switch light
- Pendant
- GPO — power outlet
- SG contact
- Ladder contact
- Salsis contact
- Buffer contacts

## CLOSEOUT

### Stage 13 — Belting & Finishes
- **Install belts** — motor, CW, cabin (permanent). Inference rule: tirak/Inex kit gear being returned to the Schindler warehouse is strong evidence this and tirak removal are done, even with no explicit "installed belts" log entry.
- **Change over mains on lift** — transition from temporary (tirak/Inex kit) power to permanent building mains
- **Remove tirak gear** *(no further breakdown yet)*
- **Remove stopblock** — comes off alongside the tirak, once permanent belting is underway (Danilo, Sep 2026) — not earlier at Stage 6, as an earlier draft assumed
- **Remove Inex kit components** *(no further breakdown yet)*
- **Pack up**
  - Stack gates/cages — fold/palletize protective gates and cages, compacted and ready for pickup
  - Break down delivery boxes
- **Clean shaft** *(no further breakdown yet)*
- **File rail joins** — where two rail lengths join, steps or protuberances sometimes need filing flush; done with either a manual filer or a grinder fitted with a flap disc
- **Install oilers** *(no further breakdown yet)*
- **Install compensation chain** — purely mechanical, done late in the job, after most other work is finished (Danilo, Sep 2026)
  - Install the chain itself
  - Install chain guides
  - Install chain safety rope
- **Remove/dismantle sky climber & sky lock** — conditional, only on jobs using a false car; closing counterpart to their Stage 1 install (Danilo, Sep 2026)
- **Final finishes/touch-ups** — includes the final strip-down: removing all protective plastic film from the finished lift before handover (cabin interior, COP, decoration ceiling, walls, doors, landing doors, landing frames, controller unit, etc.) — a distinct, later meaning of "strip" from the Stage 2 prep-strip and Rip-Out-strip senses

Reminder: belting only requires Stage 6 (bedplates/motor) + minimum controller wiring from Stage 9 — not full completion of Stages 9-12.

### Stage 14 — Commissioning & Tuning
**Controller Programming / Parameter Setup**
- Program controller parameters *(no further breakdown yet — confirmed real-world activity, e.g. "programming with [name]" in logs)*

**Final Component Tuning**
- Tune magnet reader — placement rule: "tuned" verb only; "install/prep" goes to Stage 10 instead
- Tune keepers — placement rule: "tuned" verb only; "install/prep" goes to Stage 7 instead
- Tune spear — placement rule: "tuned" verb only; "install/prep" goes to Stage 4 instead
- Tune rollers — always here; rollers arrive pre-fitted with headers, never logged as a separate install
- Tune/fix contacts (unspecified location) — "contacts" appears across many systems (safety gear, SG/ladder/buffer, bottles, foldable handrail). If the log names a specific location, place it there instead; if not, it defaults here.

**Leveling & Adjustments**
- Level/adjust car position
- Adjust door alignment/gaps
- Adjust ride comfort/smoothness settings
- Fine-tune floor-level stopping accuracy

**Testing**
- Load test
- Speed test
- Safety systems test — brakes, governor, buffers, door safety
- Final functional test/run

Note: testing is typically done in a single day by a dedicated tester (not the install crew). Logs from test day are often terse ("test lift 1") since the tester commonly leaves a separate punch-list of remaining items rather than a detailed log — treat brief test-day entries as valid, complete evidence for this bucket rather than flagging them as under-detailed.

**Certification & Handover**
- Certification inspection/paperwork
- Client walkthrough/handover
- Final documentation/as-built delivery

Note: a log simply saying "complete lift [N]" around test/handover time typically means the handover itself — covering certification, sign-off, and closing out the tester's punch-list. Place these here rather than treating them as too vague to score.

Note: generic log entries like "items list," "pending items," or "final items" — common in real logs at this phase — should default to this stage rather than being left unscoreable, since that's how installers actually write up this phase in practice.

**Exception:** if the surrounding context points to an installer handoff instead (e.g. "items list" paired with packing up tools/organizing parts "for the next guy," with no test/tuning/certification language nearby), treat it as a pre-handover punch-list, not commissioning evidence — leave unscored rather than crediting Stage 14.
